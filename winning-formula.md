## Hook Pattern (Slide 1)

```
[Entity] just [past-tense action] [timing/detail].

The reason?

[Absurd/surprising punchline].
```

## Real Examples (1M+ views)

| Post | Hook |
|------|------|
| Norway hotel swap | "Norway just swapped hotels 3 days before facing England. The reason? Construction noise and... no beach." |
| Argentina FA hacked | "Argentina's FA just got hacked. The emails called their own players 'mercenaries'." |
| Belgium vs Yamal | "Belgium manager just admitted they have 'no plan' to stop Yamal. Then said they'd try anyway." |

## Why It Works

| Element | Function | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| `just [past-tense]` | **Immediacy** — something just happened | "just swapped", "just admitted" |
| `[timing/detail]` | **Credibility** — specific & concrete | "3 days before", "45 minutes ago" |
| "The reason?" | **Curiosity gap** — forces reader to continue | Forces scroll to next slide |
| Absurd punchline | **Shareability** — funny & relatable | "no beach", "called their own players mercenaries" |

## ❌ Anti-Patterns (Kills Engagement)

| Generic (❌) | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| "hits out at" | Generic editorial, no urgency |
| "shows signs of" | Vague, no concrete action |
| "can be [team]'s answer to" | Speculative, no event happened |
| "In a recent match..." | Boring context preamble |
| Starting with the conclusion | No curiosity gap |

## ✅ Rewrite Rules

Replace vague verbs with concrete past-tense actions:

| Generic (❌) | Winning (✅) |
|---|---|
| Tuchel hits out at FIFA over Quansah ban | FIFA just doubled Quansah's ban. Tuchel got zero explanation why. |
| Yamal showing signs of Messi, Maradona | A 19-year-old just made Maradona's family compare him to Diego. |
| Arsenal wonderkid can be England's answer to Yamal | Freddy Adu just compared an Arsenal teen to Lamine Yamal. Then to Messi. |
| Belgium manager admits 'no plan' to stop Yamal | Belgium's manager just told the world they have 'no plan' for Yamal. Then smiled. |

**Pattern:** Replace vague verbs → concrete past-tense action + timing + absurd detail.

## Reference Pattern: Question + Reasons

Use when article contains multiple distinct, sourced reasons or turning points.

```text
Why did [person] walk away from [high-stakes outcome] after [advanced-stage detail]?
[Named source] revealed [supported number] key reasons behind the decision.
```

Slide progression:

1. S1 asks one specific unresolved question and promises only the number of reasons supported by source.
2. S2 gives timeline and explains how close outcome came to happening.
3. S3-S5 deliver one concrete reason or turning point per slide, with attribution preserved.
4. S6 returns to opening question and asks grounded either/or or judgment question.

Reference post progression: advanced talks → club hesitation → reported internal concern → rival club's opening → callback debate. Copy structure, never wording. Do not invent mystery, reason, count, motive, or outcome.

## 8 Viral Criteria

Hit minimum **2 criteria per slide**:

| # | Criteria | Description |
|---|----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Pro & Con** | Debate, disagreement, two sides |
| 2 | **Relatable** | Universal: money, loyalty, betrayal, ambition |
| 3 | **Famous figure** | Big name = scroll stopper |
| 4 | **Viral / trending** | Already discussed, add new angle |
| 5 | **Comedy / irony** | Funny angle, absurd twist |
| 6 | **Surprising fact** | Jaw-dropping detail that reframes story |
| 7 | **Emotional hook** | Anger, sympathy, nostalgia, frustration |
| 8 | **Absurd detail** | Weirdest/most unexpected detail. "No beach" > "unsuitable accommodation" |

## Slide Structure

| Slide | Role | Max Words | Key Rule |
|-------|------|-----------|----------|
| **S1** | Hook | 40 words | Select Question + Reasons or Immediate Action from evidence |
| **S2** | Setup | 40 words | Timeline and near-outcome |
| **S3** | Reason / tension | 40 words | One concrete sourced beat |
| **S4** | Reason / human | 40 words | One concrete sourced beat, attribution preserved |
| **S5** | Reason / unresolved | 40 words | Rival opening, consequence, or evidence gap only if sourced |
| **S6** | CTA | 40 words | Specific callback to S1, no generic bait |

## Content Rules

- Conversational English, tongue-in-cheek
- Short sentences, one idea each
- Use "just" for immediacy
- Include timing details
- Find the absurd/surprising angle
- Zero hashtags in slides (caption only)
- No em-dash, no AI throat-clearing
- Every sentence passes the "text message test"
